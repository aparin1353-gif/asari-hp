on-trade accounts per month in western Tokyo, growing distribution across champagne,
whisky and spirits brands. I received two internal awards.

dominated by a competitor. I analyzed the account on three axes — POS data, customer
footfall profile, and competitor planogram — quantified its rotation potential, and used
those numbers to build agreement with both the distributor and the store owner. The shelf
was rebuilt around our brands.

The second was for expanding a whisky brand's SKU lineup through brand experience design.
I targeted an account with real influence over the local bar scene and designed the
experience around the brand — lighting, point-of-sale tools, a bespoke menu — while
building the owner relationship that made it stick.

■ SORAMICHI Inc. | CX Consultant (Jun 2026 – present)
A consultancy specializing in digital marketing and data analytics. I support CX
improvement and analytics infrastructure for large enterprise clients.
